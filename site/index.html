The only time Leftover goes online is when you click Check for updates, and then it only asks GitHub for the latest version.

How it compares

Not all disk cleaners understand developers.

Traditional disk cleaners help you reclaim space. Leftover helps you understand what you’re cleaning, why it’s there, and whether it’s safe to remove.

Disk analyzers

WizTree, WinDirStat, TreeSize, DaisyDisk

Map every byte on a drive, fast, as a treemap or a sorted list. They show you where the space went. Deciding what each folder is, and whether it’s safe to remove, is up to you.

System cleaners

BleachBit, CCleaner, CleanMyMac

Clear browser, system and app junk from a curated list. Recent versions reach into developer territory: BleachBit can deep-scan for node_modules and venv, and CleanMyMac has a developer CLI.

Developer cleaners

DevCleaner, npkill, Mole, kondo

Know developer folders by name and by project. The best of them label risk and show when a project was last touched. Most remove things permanently or send them to the Trash.

Command-line utilities

ncdu, dust, gdu, du, docker system prune, npm cache clean

Precise, scriptable, and often already installed. Each one covers a single disk or a single tool, and you supply the knowledge of what’s safe to remove.

Leftover

A developer cleaner built around the why

Every item says what it is, which tool made it, how it comes back, and links to that tool’s own docs. Cleaning moves things into a vault you can restore from for 14 days, instead of deleting them.

Installing on your Mac

  1. Open Leftover.dmg from your Downloads and drag Leftover onto Applications.
  2. Open Leftover from Applications, Launchpad or Spotlight.
  3. The first time, macOS says it can’t verify Leftover. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

Installing on Windows

  1. Run Leftover-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
  3. Click through the installer. Leftover opens when it’s done and stays in your Start menu. No admin rights needed.

Installing on Linux

  1. Double-click the .deb to open it in your software installer and click Install.
  2. Open Leftover from your app menu.

Leftover isn’t signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, which is why your computer asks once. It’s open source, and every download is listed with its SHA-256 checksum on the release page.
