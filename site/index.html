Privacy

It never leaves your computer.

Leftover does all of its work on your machine. There is no server on the other end.

scanning ~/

Runs 100% locally.

Scanning and cleanup happen on your machine. Nothing is sent anywhere.

No account. No sign-in.

Open it and it works. There’s nothing to register and nobody to log in to.

No telemetry.

No analytics, no crash reports, no update checks. Nothing about you or your files is collected.

Works offline.

Turn off Wi‑Fi. Leftover won’t notice.

Open source.

Read every line on GitHub. Build it yourself if you’d rather.

Reversible by design.

A 14-day vault and one-click restore. Change your mind; nothing’s lost.
Our privacy policy, in short

Leftover reads folder names, paths and sizes on your computer so it can show you what’s there. The results stay in memory and in one local file you can delete. Nothing is uploaded, because there’s nowhere to upload it to.
