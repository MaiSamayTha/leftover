    Explain

    No guesswork. Every item comes with a reason.

    Leftover tells you what each folder is, which tool made it, and how it comes back. Every “safe” verdict links to the tool's own documentation. Anything it can't vouch for is shown, explained and left alone.

    Vault

    Nothing is deleted until you're sure.

    Cleared items move into a vault on the same drive. It's instant, and anything can be put back exactly where it was for 14 days. If your tools already rebuilt it, Leftover restores beside the new copy instead of overwriting it.

    Privacy

    It never leaves your computer.

    A tool that looks through your disk should be one you never have to wonder about. Leftover is a single program that runs on your machine, talks to no one, and keeps a record of every change it makes.

    Runs 100% locally

    Scanning, explaining and cleaning all happen on your machine. There is no server.

    No account

    No sign-up, no sign-in, no email. Download it and run it.

    No telemetry

    No analytics, no crash reporting, no update checks. Nothing about you or your files is collected.

    Works offline

    Everything Leftover knows is built in. Unplug the network and it works exactly the same.

    Open source

    Every line is on GitHub under the MIT license, with no dependencies beyond Go's standard library.

    Reversible by design

    A 14-day vault with one-click put back, and a history of every move, restore and deletion.

    Read the plain-English privacy statement or check the code yourself.

    Knows the tools you use.

    Each one comes with a written rule: where it keeps things, what they are, and the documentation that says they come back.
